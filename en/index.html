Software Engineering · B.Eng. alumnus & M.Eng. student · Southwest University

I study safety and reachability in stochastic systems.

I am pursuing an M.Eng. in Software Engineering at Southwest University. In my research, I ask a specific question: when a system is subject to stochastic disturbances, can its controller reach a target while avoiding unsafe regions? I work on mathematical methods that make those probabilistic guarantees checkable.

Outside research, I also turn recurring problems in study and media work into local software that I expect to keep using.
