Research & Engineering

I study safety and reachability in stochastic systems.

Under stochastic disturbance and model mismatch, can a controller reach its target while avoiding unsafe regions? I study how such probabilistic safety requirements can be stated, and which conditions support the resulting claims. My public projects document practical implementations of local AI, knowledge tools, and software systems.

Research question

Performance evidence and safety evidence answer different questions.

Training curves and simulations describe a policy in tested settings. A safety claim must also state its assumptions and the states, disturbances, and task boundaries to which it applies.
