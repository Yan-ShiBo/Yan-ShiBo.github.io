Southwest University · M.Eng. student in Software Engineering

I study how to synthesize reach-avoid controllers for stochastic systems and provide verifiable probabilistic guarantees.

Under stochastic disturbances, a system must reach its target region while avoiding unsafe regions beforehand. My work focuses on synthesizing controllers that meet this requirement and using formal methods to establish a lower bound on the probability of satisfying it.

Outside my core research, I also turn recurring problems in research, study, and content processing into software that I expect to keep using.

Current work

What I’m working on.

Since beginning graduate study, I have spent most of my time on the following three projects.

Research

Learning-aided reach-avoid controller synthesis

Starting from a reference policy obtained through reinforcement learning, I construct sparse polynomial controllers that are easier to verify. I then use stochastic barrier-like certificates to compute and iteratively improve a verifiable lower bound on the reach-avoid probability.

View the research background
Funded research

Formal safety verification of neural-network control policies

I contribute to an NSFC General Program project that studies the conditions and domains under which neural-network control policies meet specified safety requirements in nonlinear systems.

Engineering

Vision-based reinforcement-learning rover

I am responsible for the system architecture, safety constraints, and simulation training, while preserving a complete interface for later embedded deployment. This project is also a long-term testbed for evaluating learning-based control and formal safety methods.
