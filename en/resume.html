Research

Research focus

  • Background: disturbances and model uncertainty mean that a controller must complete its task while making its risk boundaries explicit.
  • Core question: how to reach a target while avoiding unsafe regions, with probabilistic guarantees that can be inspected and checked.
  • Why it matters: verifiable performance claims support the safe deployment of learning-enabled controllers in complex autonomous systems.
