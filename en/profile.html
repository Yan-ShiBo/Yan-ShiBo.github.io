My path to graduate school

During my first graduate-school entrance examination in late 2022, China had just lifted its nationwide COVID-19 restrictions. I contracted COVID-19 shortly before the exam and was not admitted to Sun Yat-sen University.

I then spent nearly a year traveling around China and reconsidering my direction and pace. In October 2023, I began preparing again, this time for the Software Engineering program at Southwest University. I was ultimately admitted back to my alma mater with the highest overall score in the program (written exam: 373; second-round assessment: 89).
